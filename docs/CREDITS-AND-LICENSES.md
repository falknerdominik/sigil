  RPG Creative (ORC) License** when the source product declares it as Licensed
  Material. ORC does **not** automatically license trademarks, story/lore,
  artwork or other Reserved Material.

  ORC. The original source books' notices determine which material is open.

  from other Pathfinder volumes and adventure paths. **Do not assume every

## Source-data notes

The bundled `spell.json` contains 1,400 spell records with source fields and
links to Archives of Nethys pages. `SPELL-SOURCES.md` summarizes the source
book titles; the original `source` fields and external references are kept
unchanged. The list identifies provenance, **not blanket redistribution
permission**.

All Sigil render and export options remain independent of attribution.
